Build/relink recipe and the separate musl compatibility header:
https://github.com/calcky/tools/tree/nettrace-release/nettrace
The nettrace source is unmodified. All source and build inputs above are
publicly available; the recipe rebuilds and relinks the complete executable.
To use a modified library, replace its archive/headers in the build image
before running the nettrace make command in build-static.sh.
